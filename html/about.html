About the project

The purpose, research, and sources behind Yunnan Mushroom Awareness.

Our project

Curiosity, culture, and care

Yunnan Mushroom Awareness explores the fungi of southwest China, the names used in local food culture, and the importance of preventing mushroom poisoning. This educational project brings our research together for curious readers.

It is not a field identification guide or a substitute for medical advice.

02 / Research notes

Questions we are still checking

Annual poisoning and death estimates

Our original notes list about 20–30 deaths and 2,786.2 poisoning cases per year. We have not established the reporting period, calculation, or case definition behind those figures, so we do not present them as confirmed annual statistics.

Original media source · August 2023 ↗

Public-health policy since 2023

The original notes mention mushroom poisoning being incorporated into disease-control work in 2023. The linked news report is a starting point; an official policy text and its exact scope still need verification.

Read the original report ↗

Common names and scientific names

Common names are not always one-to-one species names. The reading list corrects “Galerina sulcipes” to “Galerina sulciceps”. The original Heimioporus and Tricholoma links point to genus pages, so we label them at that level rather than claiming species-level support.
