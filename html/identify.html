Yunnan / 云南菌子

Check a photo before you ask anyone else.

Upload a photograph and our classifier will suggest the most likely species and what is known about its toxicity.

Read this first

A photograph cannot tell you what is safe to eat.

This tool gives a suggestion, not a verdict. Many dangerous species are near-identical to edible ones in a photo, and more than 200 poisonous species grow in Yunnan. Never eat a wild mushroom on the strength of an image match.

If someone has already eaten a mushroom and you are worried, do not wait for symptoms — call 120.
