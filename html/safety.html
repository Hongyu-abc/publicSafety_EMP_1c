Yunnan / 云南菌子

Enjoy the culture. Know the risks.

Understand why wild mushroom identification matters and what to do if poisoning is suspected.

Suspected poisoning

Get medical help immediately.

If you suspect poisonous mushrooms were eaten, seek urgent medical care. Do not wait for symptoms. In mainland China, call 120 for an ambulance.

Tell clinicians when the mushrooms were eaten and who else ate them. If available, take leftover mushrooms or photos to help identification, without delaying care.

Guidance: China CDC: mushroom poisoning · China CDC: diagnosis and identification

01 / Before you eat

Familiar-looking can still be dangerous

01

Avoid unknown wild mushrooms

Do not pick and eat wild mushrooms you cannot reliably identify. A photo, an app, or a familiar local name cannot certify a specimen as safe.

02

Colour is not a safety test

Poisonous mushrooms are not always brightly coloured. Some dangerous species are white or grey; appearance alone is unreliable.

03

Take possible exposure seriously

Symptoms can be delayed, and poisoning can become severe. Seek medical assessment after suspected ingestion even if you initially feel well.

Sources: China CDC: prevention · China CDC: emergency guidance
