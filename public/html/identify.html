Information is not an edibility decision.

Wikipedia can help you learn about a species, but it cannot confirm a wild mushroom's identity or whether it is safe to eat. Many dangerous species are near-identical to edible ones.

If someone has already eaten a mushroom and you are worried, do not wait for symptoms — call 120.
